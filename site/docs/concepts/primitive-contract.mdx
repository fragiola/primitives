---
title: The primitive contract
description: What every part of every primitive accepts and guarantees - namespaces, render instead of asChild, refs, forwarded props, structural style only, no text.
---

Every primitive follows the same contract, in each framework's idiom.

## One namespace per component

A component's parts are one namespace: `Dialog.Root`, `Dialog.Trigger`, `Dialog.Popup`, ... in
React and Vue; in Angular, one standalone directive per part, `[dialogRoot]`, `[dialogTrigger]`,
`[dialogPopup]`, applied to your own elements.

## render, never asChild

A part renders a default element (a `button` for a trigger, a `div` for a popup), and you can
replace it:

<Framework name="react">

`render={<a href="/profile" />}` merges the part's props into your element;
`render={(props, state) => ...}` receives them with the part's state. `className` and `style`
take a value or a function of the state.

</Framework>

<Framework name="vue">

`as="section"` (or a component) changes the element; the `render` scoped slot receives the part's
`props` and `state` to spread on your own element. `class` and `style` are values; style the
state through its `data-*` attributes.

</Framework>

<Framework name="angular">

The directive goes on your own element, which is the part: there is nothing to replace. A part's
`state()` is available through its `exportAs` name (`#popup="dialogPopup"`).

</Framework>

## Props, refs and handlers

- `ref` is a plain prop in React 19; in Vue a template ref gives the part's element (`$el`); in
  Angular a template reference variable does.
- Any other prop is forwarded to the element: `id`, `aria-*`, `data-*`, event handlers.
- Handlers compose: the part's own handler runs first, then yours.

## Controlled and uncontrolled

A value is controlled when you pass it (`open`, `value`) and uncontrolled otherwise (seeded by
`defaultOpen`, `defaultValue`). Every change is reported with its reason:
`onOpenChange(open, { reason })` in React, `v-model:open` and `@open-change` in Vue, `[(open)]`
and `(openChangeDetails)` in Angular. A controlled value you keep unchanged stays yours: a dialog
whose `open` you keep `true` stays open and working.

## No text, structural style only

A primitive renders only your children: no text and no accessible name of its own. Its inline
style is structural only (a position, a size, a CSS variable your styles read, like the tab
indicator's `--tabs-indicator-inline-size`), never cosmetic.
