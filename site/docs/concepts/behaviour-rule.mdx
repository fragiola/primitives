Every behaviour of every primitive comes from one rule: **the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices (APG)
first; where the APG has no rule, Base UI.**

## The APG first

The APG describes each pattern's roles, states, keyboard and focus: the [Dialog](/docs/api/dialog)
follows the modal dialog pattern, the [Tabs](/docs/api/tabs) the tabs pattern. Where it gives a
rule, the primitive follows it, even when a popular library does something else. Two examples:

- A modal dialog's popup sets `aria-modal="true"`, as the APG asks; Base UI relies on making the
  outside inert instead.
- Tabs select on focus by default (automatic activation, the APG's recommendation when a panel
  shows without delay); manual activation is an option.

## Base UI where the APG is silent

The APG says nothing of naming, animation states or many edge cases: there, the primitive follows
Base UI. That is why the attributes are `data-popup-open`, `data-starting-style` and
`data-ending-style`, and why the wrap-around prop is `loopFocus`.

## A new difference

Each decided difference is written in the component's spec with its reason, and the conformance
suite tests it in all three frameworks.
