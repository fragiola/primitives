Every behaviour of every primitive comes from one rule: **the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices (APG)
first; where the APG has no rule, Base UI.**

## The APG first

The APG describes each pattern's roles, states, keyboard and focus: the [Dialog](/docs/api/dialog)
follows the modal dialog pattern, the [Popover](/docs/api/popover) the dialog pattern without its
modal parts, the [Tooltip](/docs/api/tooltip) the tooltip pattern, the [Menu](/docs/api/menu) the
menu button pattern, the [Tabs](/docs/api/tabs) the tabs pattern, the
[Checkbox](/docs/api/checkbox) and the [Slider](/docs/api/slider) theirs, the
[Select](/docs/api/select) the select-only combobox, the [Combobox](/docs/api/combobox) the
combobox pattern, and the [Calendar](/docs/api/calendar) the grid of the date picker dialog
example. Where it gives a rule, the
primitive follows it, even when a popular library does something else. Some examples:

- A modal dialog's popup sets `aria-modal="true"`, as the APG asks; Base UI relies on making the
  outside inert instead.
- Tabs select on focus by default (automatic activation, the APG's recommendation when a panel
  shows without delay); manual activation is an option.
- A popover focuses its first tabbable element on open ("generally, focus is initially set on the
  first focusable element").
- A tooltip wires itself: its popup is a `tooltip` and its trigger's `aria-describedby` points at
  it; Base UI leaves both to you.
- A menu and a select are non-modal by default, as the APG's menu button and select-only
  combobox; `modal` gives Base UI's behaviour (scroll lock, a blocked outside pointer).
- A select opens its list below the trigger (no `alignItemWithTrigger`), keeps DOM focus on the
  trigger with `aria-activedescendant`, chooses the highlighted option on Tab, and a printable key
  on the closed trigger opens the list on the matching option: the APG select-only combobox,
  where Base UI moves focus into the list and chooses without opening.

- A calendar's Home and End go to the start and end of the week, and its weekday headers stay
  exposed, as in the APG's date picker grid. Base UI has no calendar: the pickers are non-modal
  popups like a Popover, where the APG's example is a modal dialog with OK and Cancel, because a
  choice closes them and there is nothing to confirm.

## Base UI where the APG is silent

The APG says nothing of naming, animation states or many edge cases: there, the primitive follows
Base UI. That is why the attributes are `data-popup-open`, `data-starting-style` and
`data-ending-style`, why the wrap-around prop is `loopFocus`, why the sides are `inline-start` and
`inline-end` (plus the physical `left` and `right`), and why a popover closed by a press outside
gives focus back to its trigger unless the press landed on something focusable. A combobox
filters its items, takes objects as values, resets its text on Escape and opens on a press on its
input, as Base UI does (the APG allows each of these).

## A new difference

Each decided difference is written in the component's spec with its reason, and the conformance
suite tests it in all three frameworks.
