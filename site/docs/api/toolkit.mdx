`@fragiola/primitives` is the framework-free toolkit the three framework packages build their
components on. Each framework package depends on it at the exact same version: you rarely import
it yourself, but you can, to build a primitive of your own.

## What it is, and what it is not

It holds **DOM behaviours and pure math**: functions and small classes that take elements and
options and return values or a cleanup. It never holds a component's open, selected or value
state, its lifecycle or its API, and it never imports a framework. It never touches the global
`document` or `window`, only an element's own document, so it works inside iframes and popup
windows. Its only runtime dependency is `@floating-ui/dom`, in the positioning entry.

## One entry per mechanism

Each mechanism is its own entry, so a component pulls in only the ones it uses: Dialog uses
`/overlay`, `/focus`, `/inert`, `/scroll-lock` and `/presence` (AlertDialog, built on Dialog's
parts, the same, with the focus trap's `holdOutsidePress`); Tabs uses `/collection` and
`/direction` (and `/presence` for its panels' enter and exit); Collapsible uses `/presence` alone
(its panel size and find-in-page match), Accordion `/presence`, `/collection` and `/direction`;
Popover adds `/positioning`; Tooltip uses `/hover`, `/pointer` and `/positioning`;
Menu uses `/collection`, `/pointer`, `/positioning` and, for its `modal` option, `/inert` and
`/scroll-lock`; ContextMenu, built on Menu's parts, adds the long press of `/pointer` and the
virtual anchor of `/positioning` (each in its own module, so Menu and Dialog do not pull them); Drawer, built on Dialog's parts, adds `/swipe`, which no other component imports; NavigationMenu uses `/overlay`, `/positioning`, `/hover`, `/pointer` (the hover bridge), `/focus`, `/collection`, `/direction` and `/presence`. The form controls use `/form` (the hidden inputs, the form bridges and the Field
layer), so Field's form code reaches only the components that import it: Field (and Fieldset
and Form, built on it), Checkbox, Switch and Radio use it alone; RadioGroup uses `/collection`
and `/direction` for its arrow keys; Slider adds `/range`, `/pointer` and
`/direction`; Select and Combobox add `/collection`, `/overlay` and `/positioning` (Select's `modal` option `/inert` and
`/scroll-lock`); the date pickers add `/date` to the Popover's mechanisms. Progress uses
`/range` alone, Avatar `/image` alone; Button and Separator use none. Toolbar and ToggleGroup use
the roving focus of `/collection` and `/direction` (a Toggle alone imports neither: its
composite's root handles the keys). NumberField adds `/number` to `/form` (its parsing and
formatting of localized text, its stepping, the auto repeat of its buttons and its scrub; no
other component imports it), OTPField uses `/form` (its value rules), `/collection` and
`/direction`; Meter uses `/range` alone, Label `/form` alone (its press). The root entry,
`@fragiola/primitives`, only re-exports them.

## The mechanisms

- **Overlay**: an overlay session ties a layer, the focus-out watch and the focus return of one
  open overlay together, and ends them with one call. After an outside press, a popover's focus
  returns only once the press has ended, and only when the press focused nothing
  (`returnFocus.whenUnfocused`). `openOverlaySession` takes the layer's `owner` (default: its
  `ignore` elements, the triggers), `backdrops` and `modal`, and calls `dismiss(reason, event)` with the DOM
  event that caused it (`DismissEvent`: a keyboard, pointer or focus event; `undefined`, with the
  reason `none`, when a parent overlay closed).
- **Layers**: the **tree** of a document's open overlays
  ([nested overlays](/docs/guides/nested-overlays)). `pushLayer(doc, options)` resolves the
  layer's parent when it opens: the newest open layer whose elements contain one of its `owner()`
  elements (its triggers), else the newest one containing the focused element, else none.
  `backdrops` are the elements that cover the page under the layer (a dialog's backdrop and
  viewport): a press on them is outside the layer and inside its parent. Escape goes to the newest
  layer only. An outside press keeps the deepest layer containing the press (its elements, or its
  `ignore` list) and its ancestors, and dismisses every other open layer, newest first.
  `modal: true` marks a layer that makes the page under it inert (a dialog): while it is the
  newest modal layer open, a press that is neither in it nor in one of its descendants counts as
  a press in its parent, so the layers it was opened from stay open and it, with the layers above
  them that are not its ancestors, gets `onOutsidePress` (a press on the inert page reaches no
  layer, and would otherwise dismiss them all).
  `Layer.elementsAbove()` gives the elements of the layer's descendants only (what a focus trap
  and a focus-out watch accept). When a layer is removed, its children move to its parent and
  each descendant still open gets `onParentClose()`, newest first. A tooltip, which is no layer,
  takes Escape before them (`interceptEscape`), so an enclosing dialog stays open.
- **Focus**: the tabbable elements of a container, the focus trap, the initial focus, and whether
  the last interaction was the keyboard. `trapFocus(container, allow, { holdOutsidePress })`
  (`FocusTrapOptions`): with `holdOutsidePress`, a press outside the trap and its child layers
  keeps focus where it is (its default focus change is prevented): an alert dialog's.
- **Inert and scroll lock**: everything outside some elements made inert, the pointer blocked
  outside a modal menu (the body stops taking pointer events), and the document's scroll locked
  (counted, so nested locks release in order), keeping the scrollbar gutter.
- **Presence**: the next frame and the end of an element's animations, for the
  `data-starting-style` and `data-ending-style` states.
- **Collection**: the key maps of tabs, menus, radio groups and chips, list navigation,
  typeahead and the combobox's item helpers. `radioKeyTarget(key, index, count, options)` gives
  the radio an arrow key moves the focus and the check to: ArrowDown and ArrowRight the next,
  ArrowUp and ArrowLeft the previous, whatever the orientation, ArrowLeft and ArrowRight swapped
  with `rtl`; it passes over the radios `skip` returns `true` for (the disabled ones), wraps at
  the ends with `loop`, and returns `null` for another key or an end without `loop`.
- **Positioning**: a floating element anchored to its trigger, or to a point
  (`virtualAnchor({ x, y, width?, height? }, contextElement?)`, a `VirtualAnchor` whose
  `AnchorRect` is in client coordinates: a context menu's pointer; its direction is read from
  `contextElement`) (Floating UI), on Base UI's sides:
  `top`, `bottom`, the logical `inline-start` and `inline-end`, and the physical `left` and
  `right`; a flipped side keeps the kind it was asked with. Its `direction` option (a direction,
  or a getter read at every placement) is a [DirectionProvider](/docs/api/direction)'s: the
  logical sides follow it. Without it, the anchor's computed direction, read at every placement.
- **Swipe**: `watchSwipe(element, options)` follows a press on `element` that moves 6 px
  (`SWIPE_LOCK_DISTANCE`) along the axis of its `directions` and reports it as a swipe
  (`onStart`, `onMove(movement)`, `onEnd(end)` with the release velocity in px per ms, or `null`
  when canceled): a move along the other axis, or one a scrollable element under the pointer
  takes (scroll containment), is let go; a touch that may be a swipe does not scroll the page; no
  swipe starts on a text control or an element marked `data-swipe-ignore`, nor, with the mouse, on
  an interactive element. `swipeRelease(direction, end, size, snap?)` decides the release:
  dismiss past half the size or on a flick (0.5 px per ms), or settle (on the closest snap point,
  `resolveSnapPoints` measuring them in px). `swipeMovement` damps a move past the fully open
  position, `swipeProgress` gives the share of the way to closed. A drawer's swipe
  ([Drawer](/docs/api/drawer)); a toast can reuse it.
- **Pointer and hover**: pointer drags, a submenu's safe area and hover bridge, hover intent and
  delay groups. `watchLongPress(element, { delay, moveTolerance, pointerTypes, onLongPress })`
  (`LongPressOptions`; defaults 500 ms, 10 px, touch only) calls `onLongPress(point, event)` once a
  press is held still that long; a move beyond the tolerance, a release or a cancel stops it, and
  the click and `contextmenu` that follow a long press are swallowed. It returns the cleanup.
- **Form**: the hidden inputs' form bridge (a reset, and focus on the primitive when its input is
  the form's first invalid control), the field's validity and its rules (the required rule, the
  modes), `createFieldValidator` for async `validate` (the latest run wins, a value already
  validated is reused), the Error's matching and messages, and a Form's pieces:
  `guardFormSubmit` (a capture listener that blocks a submission before any other listener),
  `firstInDocumentOrder`, `focusFieldControl`, and the server errors (`formErrorsFor`,
  `withoutFormErrors`).
- **Direction**: `getDirection(element, provided)`, the direction a provider gives, else the
  element's computed `direction`.
- **Number**: a number field's text and steps (Epic #52). `formatNumber(value, { locale, format })`
  writes a value (`""` for `null`; every decimal kept without a format); `parseNumber(text, options)`
  reads the locale's writing back: its digits (Arabic-Indic, Extended Arabic-Indic and ASCII in
  every locale), its group and decimal separators (`.` or `,` also read as the decimal point where
  the locale groups with neither), any minus or plus sign and the bidirectional marks around it,
  the currency, unit and percent text (a percent format reads `25%` as `0.25`), an accounting
  negative in parentheses; `null` for a text with no digit, `NaN` for one that is no number.
  `isNumberText` says whether a text holds only those characters (a field refuses the others as
  they are typed). `stepNumber(value, amount, { min, max, snapOnStep })` steps and clamps with no
  floating-point noise (an empty field starts at `0`). `watchAutoRepeat(button, { onStep, delay,
  interval })` steps at a press, then every 60 ms after 400 ms (a click no press started steps
  once; a mouse press keeps the focus). `watchScrub(element, { direction, pixelSensitivity,
  onStart, onScrub, onEnd, onCursor })` reports steps per pixels moved, under the pointer lock for
  a mouse, with the virtual cursor's point wrapped around the viewport.
- **Range**: slider math and keys, and a progress bar's value. `progressValue(value, options)`
  clamps the value to `[min, max]` and returns it with its percent (0 to 100), its status
  (`ProgressStatus`: `indeterminate` for `null`, `complete` at `max`, else `progressing`) and its
  formatted value: `format` and `locale` for `Intl.NumberFormat`, a percentage of the range
  without `format`, `""` when indeterminate.
- **Image**: `watchImageStatus(element, src, options, onStatus)` loads an image outside the
  document, through the element's window (a `new Image()` of its `ownerDocument.defaultView`),
  with the `img`'s `srcSet`, `sizes`, `crossOrigin` and `referrerPolicy` (`ImageSourceOptions`),
  and reports its status (`ImageLoadingStatus`): `loading`, then `loaded` or `error`. No `src`
  and no `srcSet` is an `error`. A cached image reports at once, synchronously and with no
  `loading`: `loaded`, or `error` when it has no size. It returns the cleanup, after which no status is reported. Avatar's Image is
  built on it.
- **Id, date**: document-unique ids, and plain dates with their math and locale data from `Intl`
  (imported only by the calendar and picker entries).
