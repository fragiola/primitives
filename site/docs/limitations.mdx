title: Known limitations
description: What the first release does not do yet - the rest of the catalog, other frameworks, npm packages - and what stays yours by design.
---

The first release covers the overlays, the tabs, the form controls and the date pickers. Here is what it leaves out,
on purpose or for a later release.

## Not in this release

- **Fifteen components.** Dialog, Popover, Tooltip, Menu, Tabs, Field, Checkbox, CheckboxGroup,
  Slider, Select, Combobox, Calendar, RangeCalendar, DatePicker and DateRangePicker ship today.
- **Dates are the first version's.** A plain `{ year, month, day }` in the Gregorian calendar,
  with no time and no time zone. No date field to type a date in parts, no time field, no
  multiple-date calendar, no month or year pickers and no presets yet; the pickers take no `name`
  (a hidden input of yours carries the value into a form) and no Field. They come with stage 3.
- **Field is the first version's.** It labels, describes and validates one control, with
  synchronous `validate` and three validation modes. Fieldset, Form (a form-level errors object),
  async validation, a debounce, and Field in the date pickers come with stage 1.
- **No RadioGroup, Switch, NumberField, Listbox or Autocomplete** yet: they come with later
  stages.
- **Combobox and Select**: no virtualization or async lists, no `readOnly` or `autoHighlight`, no
  input inside the popup; a Select chooses one value (no `multiple`, groups or scroll arrows), and
  a Combobox has no `modal` option.
- **No menu checkbox or radio items, context menu, menubar or hover card** yet: they come with
  later stages.
- **Not on npm yet.** The packages are published once the maintainer validates the library.
- **React, Vue and Angular.** Svelte and Solid are not planned for now.
- **No alert dialog.** A modal dialog only; an alert dialog comes with its own spec.
- **No screen reader sign-off yet.** The conformance suite checks roles, names, focus and axe in
  every framework, but a person has not yet checked each pattern with a screen reader.

## Yours by design

- **The look**: no CSS, no icons, no themes ship with the packages.
- **The words**: no text and no accessible names of their own, not even "Close".
- **Markup and CSS components**: cards, badges, alerts and spinners are Fragiola UI's, not
  primitives'.
