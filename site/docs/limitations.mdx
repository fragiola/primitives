title: Known limitations
description: What the first release does not do yet - the rest of the catalog, other frameworks, npm packages - and what stays yours by design.
---

The first release covers the overlays (a drawer swiped away included), the tabs, a navigation menu, a collapsible and an accordion, the form controls with their field layer, the
date pickers, a number field and a one-time code field, and a progress bar, a meter, an avatar and a separator. Here is what it leaves out, on purpose or for a later release.

## Not in this release

- **Fifty-two components.** Dialog, AlertDialog, Drawer, Popover, Tooltip, Menu, ContextMenu, NavigationMenu, Tabs, Collapsible, Accordion, Field, Fieldset, Form, Label,
  Button, Toggle, ToggleGroup, Toolbar, Checkbox, CheckboxGroup, RadioGroup, Radio, Switch, Slider, NumberField, OTPField, Select, Combobox, Autocomplete, Listbox, Calendar,
  RangeCalendar, MultipleCalendar, MonthCalendar, MonthRangeCalendar, YearCalendar, YearRangeCalendar, DatePicker, DateRangePicker, MonthPicker, MonthRangePicker, YearPicker, YearRangePicker, DateField, TimeField, DateRangeField, TimeRangeField, Progress, Meter, Avatar and Separator ship today, with the opt-in virtual lists of Listbox,
  Select, Combobox and Autocomplete ([ListboxVirtual](/docs/api/listbox-virtual), [SelectVirtual](/docs/api/select-virtual),
  [ComboboxVirtual](/docs/api/combobox-virtual), [AutocompleteVirtual](/docs/api/autocomplete-virtual)), the opt-in typed input of the pickers
  ([DatePickerInput](/docs/api/date-picker-input), [DateRangePickerInput](/docs/api/date-range-picker-input)), a calendar's opt-in
  [presets](/docs/api/calendar-preset) and [views](/docs/api/calendar-view), and three entries that are no
  component: the [direction provider](/docs/api/direction), the [render utilities](/docs/api/render) and the
  [async list](/docs/api/async-list).
- **Dates are stage 3's third step.** A plain `{ year, month, day }`, a plain time, a month
  `{ year, month }` and a year `{ year }` in the Gregorian calendar, with no time zone: typed in a
  DateField, a TimeField, their range fields or a picker's typed input, chosen in a calendar, a
  [MultipleCalendar](/docs/api/multiple-calendar), a [MonthCalendar](/docs/api/month-calendar) or a
  [YearCalendar](/docs/api/year-calendar) (and their pickers), with [presets](/docs/api/calendar-preset)
  and a calendar's [views](/docs/api/calendar-view). No calendar systems or time zones yet: they
  come with the last step of stage 3. A MultipleCalendar has no `required`, no Field and no
  picker; a view's month or year only navigates.
- **DateField and TimeField leave out** a paste and an IME composition into a segment, the
  expansion of a two-digit year (`26` is the year 26), a native message for an unavailable date
  (a Field's `validate` gives yours), and a range across midnight (a TimeField's `min` later than
  its `max`); a controlled value the consumer refuses leaves the typed segments shown. The range
  fields check only their ends against `isDateUnavailable` (the RangeCalendar has no
  non-contiguous ranges); a typed picker's entry out of range is no value (`null`), so a picker
  that is not `required` submits nothing for it.
- **The field layer leaves out** Base UI's `Field.Item`, `Field.Validity`, a validation debounce
  (`validationDebounceTime`: debounce your async `validate` yourself), the controlled `dirty` and
  `touched`, the Error's enter and exit animations, and the Form's `onFormSubmit` (read the native
  `submit` event and its `FormData`) and `actionsRef`. A form reset does not reset a field's
  states, as in Base UI.
- **Listbox leaves out** `aria-activedescendant` focus (DOM focus moves to the options), item
  actions, `disallowEmptySelection`, `readOnly`, and grid and tree collections (Fragiola's data-grid and tree libraries). A press on a
  `Field.Label` does not focus a Listbox (a `div` is not labelable). Nor a radio outside a group
  or a read-only RadioGroup.
- **Autocomplete leaves out** Base UI's `inline` (a list with no popup), `keepHighlight`,
  `highlightItemOnHover`, `submitOnItemClick`, `onItemHighlighted`, `autoHighlight="always"`,
  `readOnly`, and an input inside the popup; Space types (it never chooses).
- **NumberField leaves out** Base UI's `onValueCommitted`, `inputRef`, `form`, the scrub's
  `teleportDistance`, cancelable change details and the `input-paste` reason (a paste is
  `input-change`); its hidden input has `step="any"` (no step validation).
- **OTPField leaves out** Base UI's `autoSubmit`, `normalizeValue` (a `RegExp` `validationType`
  covers a pattern; uppercase in your `onValueChange`), `form` and its own `Separator`.
- **Avatar leaves out** Base UI's `keepMounted` on the Image and its enter and exit transitions:
  the image only shows once loaded.
- **Combobox and Select**: no `readOnly` or `autoHighlight`, no
  input inside the popup; a Select chooses one value (no `multiple`, scroll arrows or
  `alignItemWithTrigger`), and a Combobox has no `modal` option.
- **The virtual lists** are flat and vertical: no virtual groups (Group, GroupLabel and Separator
  are not rendered in them), no horizontal list, grid or tree, no virtual Menu or command palette.
  Their rows need a flex or grid column with no margin of their own (the virtualizer writes the
  rows' margins), and `itemSize` should be the typical row height, so the scrollbar is right
  before the rows are measured. The async list has no sorting.
- **No hover card or toast** yet: they come with later stages. Value changes
  (`onValueChange`, `onCheckedChange`) cannot be canceled; only open changes can.
- **Drawer leaves out** Base UI's `Provider` and indent effect, the `SwipeArea` (swipe to open),
  the virtual keyboard provider, `snapToSequentialPoints`, a non-modal drawer and
  `disablePointerDismissal`; snap points are vertical only, and have no keys (the keyboard reaches
  the content and closes the drawer).
- **NavigationMenu leaves out** the lifecycle controls (`actionsRef`, `onOpenChangeComplete`,
  `keepMounted`), the Link's `closeOnClick`, an inline nested level without a Portal, and the
  Positioner's `collisionAvoidance`, `sticky` and `positionMethod`.
- **Not on npm yet.** The packages are published once the maintainer validates the library.
- **React, Vue and Angular.** Svelte and Solid are not planned for now.
- **No screen reader sign-off yet.** The conformance suite checks roles, names, focus and axe in
  every framework, but a person has not yet checked each pattern with a screen reader.

## Yours by design

- **The look**: no CSS, no icons, no themes ship with the packages.
- **The words**: no text and no accessible names of their own, not even "Close".
- **Markup and CSS components**: cards, badges, alerts and spinners are Fragiola UI's, not
  primitives'.
