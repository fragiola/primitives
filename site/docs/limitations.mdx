---
title: Known limitations
description: What the first release does not do yet - the rest of the catalog, other frameworks, npm packages - and what stays yours by design.
---

The first release covers the overlays (a drawer swiped away included), the tabs, a navigation menu, a collapsible and an accordion, the form controls with their field layer, the
date pickers, and a progress bar, an avatar and a separator. Here is what it leaves out, on purpose or for a later release.

## Not in this release

- **Thirty-three components.** Dialog, AlertDialog, Drawer, Popover, Tooltip, Menu, ContextMenu, NavigationMenu, Tabs, Collapsible, Accordion, Field, Fieldset, Form,
  Button, Toggle, ToggleGroup, Toolbar, Checkbox, CheckboxGroup, RadioGroup, Radio, Switch, Slider, Select, Combobox, Calendar,
  RangeCalendar, DatePicker, DateRangePicker, Progress, Avatar and Separator ship today, with two entries that are no component: the
  [direction provider](/docs/api/direction) and the [render utilities](/docs/api/render).
- **Dates are the first version's.** A plain `{ year, month, day }` in the Gregorian calendar,
  with no time and no time zone. No date field to type a date in parts, no time field, no
  multiple-date calendar, no month or year pickers and no presets yet. They come with stage 3.
- **The field layer leaves out** Base UI's `Field.Item`, `Field.Validity`, a validation debounce
  (`validationDebounceTime`: debounce your async `validate` yourself), the controlled `dirty` and
  `touched`, the Error's enter and exit animations, and the Form's `onFormSubmit` (read the native
  `submit` event and its `FormData`) and `actionsRef`. A form reset does not reset a field's
  states, as in Base UI.
- **No NumberField, Listbox or Autocomplete** yet: they come with later stages. Nor a toggle
  button, a Meter, a radio outside a group or a read-only RadioGroup.
- **Avatar leaves out** Base UI's `keepMounted` on the Image and its enter and exit transitions:
  the image only shows once loaded.
- **Combobox and Select**: no virtualization or async lists, no `readOnly` or `autoHighlight`, no
  input inside the popup; a Select chooses one value (no `multiple`, scroll arrows or
  `alignItemWithTrigger`), and a Combobox has no `modal` option.
- **No hover card or toast** yet: they come with later stages. Value changes
  (`onValueChange`, `onCheckedChange`) cannot be canceled; only open changes can.
- **Drawer leaves out** Base UI's `Provider` and indent effect, the `SwipeArea` (swipe to open),
  the virtual keyboard provider, `snapToSequentialPoints`, a non-modal drawer and
  `disablePointerDismissal`; snap points are vertical only, and have no keys (the keyboard reaches
  the content and closes the drawer).
- **NavigationMenu leaves out** the lifecycle controls (`actionsRef`, `onOpenChangeComplete`,
  `keepMounted`), the Link's `closeOnClick`, an inline nested level without a Portal, and the
  Positioner's `collisionAvoidance`, `sticky` and `positionMethod`.
- **Not on npm yet.** The packages are published once the maintainer validates the library.
- **React, Vue and Angular.** Svelte and Solid are not planned for now.
- **No screen reader sign-off yet.** The conformance suite checks roles, names, focus and axe in
  every framework, but a person has not yet checked each pattern with a screen reader.

## Yours by design

- **The look**: no CSS, no icons, no themes ship with the packages.
- **The words**: no text and no accessible names of their own, not even "Close".
- **Markup and CSS components**: cards, badges, alerts and spinners are Fragiola UI's, not
  primitives'.
