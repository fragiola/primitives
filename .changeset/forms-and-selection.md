Field, Checkbox, CheckboxGroup, Slider, Select and Combobox, natively in React, Vue and Angular, one entry each (`/field`, `/checkbox`, `/checkbox-group`, `/slider`, `/select`, `/combobox`), with form support: hidden inputs, native submit, reset and validation, and the Field layer. Select keeps DOM focus on its trigger with `aria-activedescendant`, Tab chooses the highlighted option, a printable key on the closed trigger opens the list on the match, and a `modal` option (non-modal by default) locks the scroll and blocks the outside pointer. Combobox filters its `items`, takes object values and `multiple` with chips, and resets its text on Escape. The toolkit's collection entry gains `scrollIntoViewWithin`.
