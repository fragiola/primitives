Tags, natively in React, Vue and Angular, each in its own entry. `/tag-group` (`Root`, `Label`, `List`, `Tag`, `TagCell`, `RemoveButton`): React Aria's grid of tags (a `grid` of `row`s with one `gridcell` each, a `group` while empty), one tab stop, the arrows of both axes (mirrored in RTL), Home and End, `loopFocus`, `selectionMode` `none`, `single` or `multiple` (Space, Enter, a press), `disabledKeys` and disabled tags that never take focus, `onRemove` from Delete, Backspace or a RemoveButton with the focus moved to the next tag, else the previous one, else the List, `data-empty`, and the Field layer's label and description. `/tags-input` (`Root`, `Control`, `Input`, `Tag`, `TagText`, `TagRemove`, `TagInput`, `ClearTrigger`): a text input whose Enter, `delimiter` and pasted lists add trimmed tags, refused as duplicates (unless `allowDuplicates`), past `max` or by `validate` (`onValueInvalid` with its `cause`), the tags reached with Backspace and the arrows and removed with Backspace or Delete, `editable` tags edited in place, `blurBehavior` (`"add"`, `"clear"`), a controlled text (`inputValue`), one hidden input per tag for native forms (submit, reset, `required`) and the Field layer. The toolkit gains the tags' keys in `/collection` (`tagGroupKeyTarget`, `removalFocusIndex`, `tagsInputInputKeyTarget`, `tagsInputTagKeyTarget`) and their rules in `/form` (`takeTags`, `editTag`, `typesDelimiter`). Every existing entry is unchanged. Choices, in their own entries too. `/segmented-control` (`Root`, `Item`, `ItemText`, `Indicator`): the APG radio group (a `radiogroup` of `radio` Items, one tab stop, every arrow moves and checks, RTL, `loopFocus`, `orientation`, disabled Items skipped) with an Indicator whose ten `--segment-indicator-*` variables (logical and physical, from the Root's padding box) follow the checked Item on a value change, a resize and the direction, `renderBeforeHydration` and `nonce` as Tabs', a hidden input for native forms (submit, reset, `required`) and the Field layer. `/rating` (`Root`, `Item`, `HiddenInput`): the APG's rating radio group (a `radio` per star, named by the app), `count`, `allowHalf` (half steps by the keys and by the inline-start half of a star, RTL included), `allowClear`, `readOnly`, `disabled`, a pointer preview (`highlighted`, `half`), arrows stepping the value with Home and End, the Root's and the Item's children functions, native forms and the Field layer. The toolkit gains `segmentIndicatorBox` and `placeSegmentIndicator` in `/collection` and `ratingKeyTarget`, `ratingValueAt` and `ratingItemState` in `/range`.
