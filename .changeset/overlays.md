Popover, Tooltip and Menu (with submenus), natively in React, Vue and Angular, one entry each (`/popover`, `/tooltip`, `/menu`). Popover focuses its first tabbable element and, after a press outside, returns focus to its trigger unless the press focused something; Tooltip wires `role="tooltip"` and `aria-describedby` and stays open while hovered; Menu is non-modal by default with a `modal` option. The toolkit's sides follow Base UI: `top`, `bottom`, `inline-start`, `inline-end`, `left`, `right`.
