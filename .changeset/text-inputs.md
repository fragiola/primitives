Text inputs, natively in React, Vue and Angular, each in its own entry. `/password-input` (`Root`, `Input`, `VisibilityTrigger`): a native input whose `type` switches between `password` and `text`, `visible`, `defaultVisible` and `onVisibleChange`, the trigger an APG toggle button (`aria-pressed`, `aria-controls`) whose pointer press keeps the focus in the Input and whose change keeps its selection, the text hidden again on a form submission and reset, `autoComplete` and every attribute passed through, native forms and the Field layer. `/search-field` (`Root`, `Input`, `ClearButton`): React Aria's search field, `type="search"`, Escape clearing a query and left to the page on an empty field, Enter calling `onSubmit` (Angular `(valueSubmit)`), a ClearButton out of the tab order that focuses the Input, native forms and the Field layer. `/clipboard` (`Root`, `Trigger`, `Indicator`, `Input`): a copy with the asynchronous Clipboard API, `timeout`, `onStatusChange` (`idle`, `copied`, `failed`), a polite live region with the app's `announcement` and `failedAnnouncement`, and on a refused write the Input focused with its text selected (no `execCommand` fallback). `/editable` (`Root`, `Area`, `Preview`, `Input`, `EditTrigger`, `SubmitTrigger`, `CancelTrigger`): a text edited in place, `activationMode` (`focus`, `click`, `dblclick`, `none`), `submitMode` (`enter`, `blur`, `both`, `none`), `onValueCommit` and `onValueRevert`, Escape reverting, the focus returned to the EditTrigger or the Preview, `placeholder`, `maxLength`, `readOnly`, `disabled`, an `input` or a `textarea`, native forms and the Field layer. The change reasons gain `submit-press` and `cancel-press`. The toolkit gains `/clipboard` (`writeClipboard`, `announceText`). Every existing entry is unchanged.
