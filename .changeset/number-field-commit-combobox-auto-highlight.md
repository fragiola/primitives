NumberField reports the committed value: `onValueCommitted` (Vue `@value-committed`, Angular `(valueCommitted)`) is called once a change is complete, on blur after typing, at a key or wheel step, at the release of a button press or a scrub. The toolkit's `watchAutoRepeat` takes an `onEnd` callback.

Combobox takes `autoHighlight`: typing highlights the first item the text keeps, so Enter chooses it, as Autocomplete's.
