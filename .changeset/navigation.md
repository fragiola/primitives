Navigation, natively in React, Vue and Angular, each in its own entry. `/link` (`Link`): the APG link on your anchor or your router's link (React `render`, Vue `as`, Angular on `routerLink`'s anchor), `current` (`aria-current="page"`) and `disabled` (`aria-disabled`, still a tab stop, its click stopped in its capture phase so no router navigates); no router dependency. `/breadcrumbs` (`Breadcrumbs`: `Root`, `List`, `Item`, `Link`, `Current`, `Separator`, `Ellipsis`): the APG breadcrumb, a named `nav` of an ordered list, separators hidden from assistive technology, an Ellipsis you compose with a Menu's trigger. `/steps` (`Steps`: `Root`, `List`, `Item`, `Trigger`, `Indicator`, `Separator`, `Content`, `PrevTrigger`, `NextTrigger`, `CompletedContent`): a process in `count` steps, an ordered list with `aria-current="step"`, `step` / `defaultStep` / `onStepChange` (reasons `trigger-press`, `previous-press`, `next-press`), the completed state at `count`, `linear` with `isStepValid`, `orientation`. `/carousel` (`Carousel`: `Root`, `ItemGroup`, `Item`, `PrevTrigger`, `NextTrigger`, `IndicatorGroup`, `Indicator`, `AutoplayTrigger`): the APG carousel, basic or tabbed (`variant`), native CSS scroll snapping, `slidesPerPage`, `slidesPerMove`, `loop`, `orientation`, `spacing`, a mouse drag, `page` and `playing` controlled or not, a rotation every `delay` that focus entering stops and a hover pauses (keeping the time it had left), `aria-live` off while rotating, RTL. Two new change reasons, `autoplay` and `focus-in`. The toolkit gains `/carousel` (the pages, the scroll to a page and the page a scroll settled on, the mouse drag, the hover and focus watch and the rotation's delay). Every existing component entry is unchanged.
