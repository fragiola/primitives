Overlays to production, natively in React, Vue and Angular. A tree of nested overlays: an overlay opened from another one's popup is its child, so outside presses, Escape, focus traps and focus-out watches work across a Popover inside a Dialog, menus opened from popovers, and portals, and closing an overlay closes the ones opened from it (the toolkit's `/layers`: `owner`, `backdrops`, `onParentClose`). Every overlay's `onOpenChange` receives `OpenChangeDetails` (`reason`, the new `imperative-action` reason, `event`, `trigger`, `cancel()`, `isCanceled`, `preventUnmountOnClose()`), and every overlay gains `onOpenChangeComplete`, imperative actions (`close()`, `unmount()`: React `actionsRef`, Vue the Root's template ref, Angular the root directive), the Portal's `keepMounted` and `data-closed`. Detached triggers for Dialog, AlertDialog, Popover, Menu and Tooltip: `createHandle()` (`create<Component>Handle`), triggers anywhere with `handle`, `payload` and `id`, the active trigger's payload in the Root's content, `triggerId` / `defaultTriggerId`, and the handle's `open`, `openWithPayload`, `close` and `isOpen`. Dialog gains a `Viewport` part, `data-nested`, `data-nested-dialog-open`, `--nested-dialogs` and Backdrop `forceRender`. New entries `/alert-dialog` (`role="alertdialog"`, no outside dismissal, focus on the least destructive action, built on Dialog's parts) and `/context-menu` (right-click at the pointer, a 500 ms touch long press, Shift+F10 and the ContextMenu key, built on Menu's parts). Menu gains `CheckboxItem`, `CheckboxItemIndicator`, `RadioGroup`, `RadioItem` and `RadioItemIndicator`; Select gains `Group`, `GroupLabel`, `Separator`, `ItemText` and an optional `items`. The toolkit gains `virtualAnchor` (`/positioning`), `watchLongPress` (`/pointer`) and `trapFocus`'s `holdOutsidePress` (`/focus`); `anchorFloating` no longer flips a top or bottom placement's start and end twice in RTL. The Angular portal no longer reads `document` on a server.
